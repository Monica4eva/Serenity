Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Pilot version

Project Serenity is in a pilot phase in Botswana. These terms will be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch.

1. About Serenity

Project Serenity is a recovery and wellbeing support app for people recovering from substance use, and for the counsellors, family and friends who support them. By creating an account or using the app you agree to these Terms and to our Privacy Policy.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. Keep your login details and PIN private; you are responsible for activity on your account.

3. Not a medical or emergency service

Serenity is a support tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. The button colours and suggestions are prompts for support, not medical assessments. Messages and alerts may be delayed or missed. In an emergency call 997 (ambulance) or 999 (police), or go to your nearest hospital.

4. Counsellors and supporters

Counsellors are responsible for their own professional practice and safeguarding. A "verified" badge means the Serenity team checked their registration details; it is not a recommendation. Only add people who have agreed to be contacted. You can stop sharing with anyone at any time.

5. Using Serenity respectfully

Do not misuse the app, try to access other people's information, or send abusive messages. We may suspend accounts that put others at risk.

6. Content

Recipes, activities and guidance are general information, not personal medical or nutrition advice. What you write remains yours.

7. Changes and ending

We may update the app and these Terms; we will tell you about important changes. You can delete your account at any time in Privacy & Data.

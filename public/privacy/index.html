Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Pilot version

Project Serenity is in a pilot phase in Botswana. This policy describes how the pilot works today. It will be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch, and we will tell you about any important changes.

1. Who we are

Project Serenity ("Serenity", "we") is a recovery and wellbeing support app for people recovering from substance use, built by Team Serenity in Gaborone, Botswana. It is available in English and Setswana. Contact: teamserenitybw@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

a. Account information

  • Your name or the name you would like to be called, your email address and, if you choose, a phone number.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google account ID. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Your language, role in the app (person in recovery, counsellor, or family and friends) and accessibility settings.

b. Recovery and wellbeing information you choose to enter

  • Check-ins (for example mood, cravings, Hungry/Angry/Lonely/Tired), sleep, meals, activity and meetings.
  • Recovery details, goals, triggers, your safety plan and your support contacts.
  • Journal entries and voice notes. Journal text is encrypted on your device before it is stored online, so we cannot read it.
  • Safety events, such as when the help button changed colour or when you opened the emergency screen, recorded with as little detail as possible.

c. Information from counsellors and supporters you invite

  • Messages, care plans and the role and name of the people you connect with. Counsellors' private session notes are visible only to that counsellor.

d. Technical information

  • If you turn on phone alerts, a notification token for your device. Basic error and security logs kept by our hosting providers.

We do not use advertising trackers and we do not collect your location in the background.

3. How we use your information

  • To run the app: sign you in, save your progress and keep it in sync across your devices.
  • To support you: show trends, suggest activities and turn the help button orange, pink or red when signs suggest you may need support.
  • To share with the people you choose, only what you allow (see section 4).
  • To measure whether Serenity helps, using anonymous totals only. Any group smaller than 10 people is hidden, so no one can be identified.
  • To keep the service safe and secure, and to meet legal obligations.

We never sell your information, never use it for advertising, and never share it with employers or insurers.

4. When information is shared

  • People you invite. You choose exactly what a counsellor, family member or friend can see, and you can change it or stop sharing at any time. Family and friends only ever see a simple status and milestones, never your journal or details. Counsellors see only what you allow, and journal entries only if you share them one by one.
  • Emergency contacts. Only if you give permission.
  • Service providers who store and run the app for us under contract: Supabase (database and sign-in, stored in the European Union, Frankfurt), Expo (app hosting and updates), Vercel (the team's admin website) and Google (only for "Sign in with Google").
  • The law. If required by law, or to protect someone's life in an emergency.

Serenity's team can see anonymous totals and manage counsellor checks and helpline numbers. The team cannot read journals, check-ins or messages.

5. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored on your device and in our database in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). This means it is transferred outside Botswana. We use providers with strong security and data-protection commitments, and we will review this arrangement under the Botswana Data Protection Act before public launch.

6. How we protect it

  • Journal text is encrypted on your device. Data on your phone is encrypted, and you can add a PIN lock.
  • All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Database rules make sure each person can only reach their own information.
  • Team access requires a second sign-in step (an authenticator app), and every team action is logged.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account in the app (Privacy & Data), your account and records are deleted from our database. Backups held by our providers are removed on their normal schedule. Anonymous totals that cannot identify you may be kept.

8. Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your information, withdraw consent, and object to how we use it. Most of this can be done in the app under Privacy & Data. For anything else, email teamserenitybw@gmail.com. You may also contact Botswana's Information and Data Protection Commission.

9. Age

Serenity is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

We will tell you in the app about important changes to this policy and, where needed, ask for your agreement again.

Pholisi ya Sephiri

Letlha la go simolola: 15 Ngwanatsele 2026 · Kgatiso ya teko

Rona. Project Serenity ke app ya go tshegetsa go fola le boitekanelo, e e dirilweng ke Team Serenity kwa Gaborone. Ikgolaganye le rona: teamserenitybw@gmail.com.

Se re se kokoanyang. Leina la gago, imeile, nomoro ya mogala (fa o batla), puo le dithulaganyo. Fa o tsena ka Google, re amogela leina, imeile le nomoro ya akhaonto ya Google fela. Ga re bone lefoko la gago la sephiri la Google. Le ditlhatlhobo, boroko, dijo, maikaelelo, leano la pabalesego, batshegetsi ba gago le dikwalo tse o di tsenyang. Dikwalo di sirelediwa (encrypted) mo sedirisweng sa gago, ka jalo ga re kgone go di bala.

Se re se dirisang. Go tsamaisa app, go go tshegetsa, go abelana fela se o se letlang, le go lekanya fa Serenity e thusa ka dipalo tse di sa supeng ope. Ga re rekise dintlha tsa gago, ga re di dirisetse dipapatso, mme ga re di abelane le bahiri kgotsa dikhampani tsa inshorense.

Go abelana. Fela le batho ba o ba laleditseng, ka se o se letlang, mme o ka emisa nako nngwe le nngwe. Ba lelapa le ditsala ba bona fela maemo a a motlhofo le dikgato. Le batlamedi ba ditirelo (Supabase kwa Frankfurt, Expo, Vercel, Google mo go tseneng fela), le fa molao o batla jalo kgotsa go sireletsa botshelo.

Kwa di bolokwang teng. Mo sedirisweng sa gago le mo polokelong ya rona kwa Lekgotleng la Yuropa (Frankfurt, Jeremane), kwa ntle ga Botswana.

Tshireletso. Dikwalo di sirelediwa mo sedirisweng; dikgolagano tsotlhe di sirelediwa; mongwe le mongwe o bona dintlha tsa gagwe fela; setlhopha se tsena ka kgato ya bobedi, mme ditiro tsotlhe di kwalwa.

Ditshwanelo tsa gago. O ka bona, baakanya, ntsha kgotsa phimola dintlha tsa gago mo app (Sephiri le Tshedimosetso), kgotsa o re kwalele. Fa o phimola akhaonto, dintlha tsa gago di a phimolwa.

Dingwaga. Serenity ke ya batho ba ba dingwaga di le 18 le go feta.

Pholisi eno e tla sekasekwa ke mmueledi pele app e phatlalatswa. Fa go na le phetogo e kgolo, re tla go itsise mo app.
